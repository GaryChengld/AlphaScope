# Multi-Timeframe Pullback Swing Trading Strategy — Entry Checklist

## MANDATORY CONDITIONS (Must Have ALL)
*If ANY mandatory condition fails, DO NOT ENTER the trade*

- [ ] **EMA 200 trend filter** — Price is trading above the 200-day EMA on the daily chart
- [ ] **Bullish EMA alignment** — EMA 20 > EMA 50 > EMA 200 on the daily chart
- [ ] **Weekly trend confirmation** — Price is above the weekly EMA 50 and weekly structure shows higher highs and higher lows
- [ ] **Valid pullback exists** — Price has retraced at least 1x ATR(14) but no more than 3x ATR(14) from the recent 20-bar high
- [ ] **Pullback to key support** — Price has pulled back to within 1x ATR of the EMA 20, EMA 50, or a Fibonacci 38.2%–61.8% retracement level
- [ ] **Market regime is favorable** — S&P 500 (SPY) is trading above its own 200-day EMA
- [ ] **No earnings within 5 trading days** — Upcoming earnings report will not interfere with the trade

**If all 7 mandatory conditions are met, proceed to scoring section.**

### SECTION 1: TREND STRENGTH — Daily Chart (Max 25 points)

**EMA Alignment Quality**
- [ ] EMA 20 > EMA 50 > EMA 200, all three sloping upward = 10 points
- [ ] EMA 20 > EMA 50 > EMA 200, but EMA 200 is flat = 7 points
- [ ] EMA 20 > EMA 50 > EMA 200, but EMA 20 and EMA 50 are converging = 4 points

**Uptrend Duration**
- [ ] 3+ months of consistent higher highs and higher lows = 8 points
- [ ] 2–3 months of uptrend = 5 points
- [ ] 6–8 weeks of uptrend = 3 points

**Relative Strength vs S&P 500**
- [ ] Stock has outperformed SPY over the past 63 trading days (RS ratio > 1.2) = 7 points
- [ ] Stock has slightly outperformed SPY (RS ratio 1.0–1.2) = 4 points
- [ ] Stock has matched SPY (RS ratio 0.9–1.0) = 2 points
- [ ] Stock has underperformed SPY (RS ratio < 0.9) = 0 points

---

### SECTION 2: PULLBACK QUALITY — Daily Chart (Max 25 points)

**Pullback Depth (Fibonacci Retracement of Last Swing)**
- [ ] 38.2%–50.0% retracement (ideal golden zone) = 10 points
- [ ] 50.0%–61.8% retracement (deeper but still valid) = 7 points
- [ ] 23.6%–38.2% retracement (shallow pullback) = 5 points
- [ ] 61.8%–78.6% retracement (deep, higher risk) = 2 points

**Pullback Depth (ATR-Based)**
- [ ] 1.5–2.5x ATR from recent high (ideal range) = 5 points
- [ ] 1.0–1.5x ATR from recent high (shallow) = 3 points
- [ ] 2.5–3.0x ATR from recent high (deep) = 2 points

**Volume During Pullback**
- [ ] Volume declining progressively each day, final day below 80% of 20-day average = 7 points
- [ ] Volume below 20-day average but not consistently declining = 4 points
- [ ] Volume roughly at 20-day average (flat) = 2 points
- [ ] Volume increasing during pullback (distribution warning) = 0 points

**Pullback Duration**
- [ ] 3–8 trading days (healthy consolidation) = 3 points
- [ ] 1–2 trading days (very shallow, may not be done) = 1 point
- [ ] 9–15 trading days (extended, but still holding support) = 2 points
- [ ] 15+ trading days (trend may be weakening) = 0 points

### SECTION 3: SUPPORT CONFLUENCE — Daily Chart (Max 15 points)

**Primary Support Level**
- [ ] Price bouncing from EMA 20 (within 0.5x ATR) = 5 points
- [ ] Price bouncing from EMA 50 (within 0.5x ATR) = 4 points
- [ ] Price bouncing from prior swing high acting as support (polarity flip) = 4 points
- [ ] Price bouncing from a round number (\$50, \$100, \$150, \$200, etc.) = 2 points

**Support Confluence (Multiple Levels Overlapping)**
- [ ] 3 or more support factors converging at the same price zone = 10 points
- [ ] 2 support factors converging (e.g., EMA 20 + Fibonacci 38.2%) = 7 points
- [ ] 1 support factor only = 3 points

*Note: Score the PRIMARY support level + the CONFLUENCE bonus. Max 15 points total for this section.*

### SECTION 4: RSI & MOMENTUM — Daily Chart (Max 10 points)

**RSI(14) Reading**
- [ ] RSI between 40–50 (pulled back from overbought, ideal entry zone) = 5 points
- [ ] RSI between 50–55 (mild pullback, still acceptable) = 3 points
- [ ] RSI between 35–40 (deeper pullback, watch for trend failure) = 2 points
- [ ] RSI below 35 or above 60 (not ideal for pullback entry) = 0 points

**RSI Structure**
- [ ] RSI showing bullish divergence (price lower low, RSI higher low) = 5 points
- [ ] RSI holding above 40 and turning upward = 3 points
- [ ] RSI flat or still declining = 0 points

### SECTION 5: 1-HOUR CHART TRIGGER SIGNAL (Max 20 points)

*Only evaluate this section after Sections 1–4 score at least 40 points combined*

**Trigger Type (pick the best one that applies)**
- [ ] Descending trendline break: 1H candle CLOSES above the pullback's descending trendline = 8 points
- [ ] Structure break: Price breaks above the most recent 1H swing high (higher high confirmed) = 8 points
- [ ] EMA crossover: 9 EMA crosses above 21 EMA on the 1-hour chart = 6 points
- [ ] Bullish engulfing or hammer/pin bar at the daily support zone on the 1H chart = 7 points
- [ ] Bullish RSI divergence on 1H chart + price breaks above the high between the two lows = 7 points
- [ ] No clear trigger signal yet = 0 points (DO NOT ENTER — wait or skip)

**Volume on Trigger Signal**
- [ ] Trigger candle/bar has volume 50%+ above 1H average volume = 5 points
- [ ] Trigger candle/bar has volume 20–50% above 1H average = 3 points
- [ ] Trigger candle/bar has volume at or near 1H average = 1 point
- [ ] Trigger candle/bar has volume below 1H average = 0 points

**Trigger Timing**
- [ ] Trigger occurred within 1–2 trading days of daily pullback signal = 4 points
- [ ] Trigger occurred on day 3 after daily pullback signal = 2 points
- [ ] Trigger occurred after day 3 (setup may be stale) = 0 points

**Trigger Time of Day (US Market Hours)**
- [ ] Signal between 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM EST (optimal session) = 3 points
- [ ] Signal between 2:00 PM – 3:30 PM EST (acceptable) = 2 points
- [ ] Signal in first 30 minutes or last 15 minutes of session = 0 points

*Note: Pick ONE trigger type + volume + timing + time of day. Max 20 points total for this section.*

**Sector Strength**

- [ ] Stock's sector ETF is underperforming the broad market = 0 points
